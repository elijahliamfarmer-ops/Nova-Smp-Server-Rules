NOVASMP

Official Server Rules, Punishment Guide & Staff Governance

Prepared by: ElijahTheElf
Version: 3.2
Updated: November 11, 2026
Applies to: NovaSMP Minecraft server, official Discord, official events, and official NovaSMP spaces.

Core standard: Play fairly. Treat people respectfully. Protect the server. Staff are held to the same player rules and additional staff standards.

How to use this rulebook

This rulebook is organized into tabs. Use the tabs to move between player rules, gameplay, punishments, staff conduct, and governance. The punishment schedule is the standard starting point; staff must still consider context, evidence, intent, impact, and valid prior offenses.

Important definitions

  • Warning: A recorded or unrecorded notice that behavior must stop. A formal warning is entered in the moderation log.
  • Kick: Removal from the current server session. A kick is not automatically a ban and does not, by itself, count as a formal warning unless staff record it as one.
  • Mute: Restriction on chat or communication for a stated duration.
  • Temporary ban: Removal from the server or Discord for a stated duration.
  • Permanent-ban review: Leadership reviews whether a permanent ban is justified. It is not automatic unless the schedule says the action is an immediate permanent ban pending review.
  • Verified offense: A violation supported by reliable evidence or a reasonable staff investigation, not merely an accusation.
  • Active eligible staff: A staff member currently serving, not on approved leave or suspended, and not disqualified from a particular vote due to a direct conflict of interest.

Scope and fairness

Rules apply to every player and staff member in official NovaSMP spaces. Conduct on an outside platform may be considered only when it is directly connected to NovaSMP, such as coordinated harassment of community members, impersonation of NovaSMP staff, or attacks on NovaSMP systems. Staff should not claim general authority over unrelated private conversations.

Ignorance of a published rule does not automatically excuse a violation. However, unclear wording must be interpreted reasonably and consistently. New or stricter rules must not be applied retroactively to conduct that occurred before the rule took effect.

Enforcement principles

  1. Protect players and server stability first.
  2. Use the least severe penalty that reasonably stops the conduct, unless the schedule or severity requires stronger action.
  3. Base decisions on evidence, not rank, popularity, donations, friendships, or personal disputes.
  4. Record significant actions and give the affected player a clear reason when doing so is safe and practical.
  5. A player may appeal. Staff must not retaliate for a good-faith appeal or report.

Rulebook status

This is the proposed NovaSMP rulebook version 3.1. Amendments are subject to the staff voting procedure in the Staff & Governance tab. Publish the approved version and effective date in the official Discord and server information channels.

Player Rules: Conduct, Chat & Safety

1. Chat and communication

1.1 — Spam and flooding

Do not flood chat with repeated messages, excessive characters, disruptive caps, repeated mentions, copy-paste spam, or rapid-fire messages that interfere with conversation.

1.2 — Harassment and targeted humiliation

Repeatedly targeting, insulting, humiliating, intimidating, or harassing another player is prohibited. This includes repeatedly directing insulting memes, images, nicknames, or jokes at a specific person after they have made it clear the behavior is unwanted.

A single disagreement, ordinary joke, or good-faith criticism is not automatically harassment. Staff must consider context, intent, repetition, impact, and whether the behavior continued after a clear request to stop.

1.3 — Hate speech and discrimination

Slurs, hateful or discriminatory attacks, and content targeting protected characteristics are prohibited. Saying it was a joke does not automatically excuse it.

1.4 — Profanity

Mild incidental profanity is not automatically a punishable offense. Directed profanity, excessive profanity, or profanity used to insult or intimidate another person may be punished under the schedule. Slurs and hateful language are handled under the hate-speech rule, not as ordinary swearing.

1.5 — Threats and intimidation

Threatening to harm, stalk, expose, or intimidate another person is prohibited. Credible real-world threats must be escalated to a Head Admin or Owner immediately.

1.6 — Respecting a stop request

If someone clearly asks you to stop directing jokes, images, nicknames, or comments at them, stop. Continuing may be treated as targeted harassment.

1.7 — Chat-filter and moderation evasion

Do not intentionally bypass chat filters, evade blocked words, use alternate spellings to post prohibited content, or continue prohibited conduct through signs, books, renamed items, private messages, or other channels.

1.8 — Impersonation

Do not impersonate staff, server leadership, or another player to deceive people, obtain privileges or items, or damage someone’s reputation. Parody that cannot reasonably mislead people is not automatically impersonation.

1.9 — Advertising and solicitation

Advertising unrelated servers, Discord communities, products, services, or social accounts without permission is prohibited. Do not repeatedly solicit players through public chat or unsolicited direct messages.

1.10 — Respectful disagreement

Criticism and respectful disagreement are allowed. Players may question a punishment or staff decision through the appeal process. Do not use disagreement as an excuse for harassment, spam, threats, or disruption.

2. Privacy and account safety

2.1 — No doxxing

Do not publish, threaten to publish, or solicit another person’s private information, including addresses, phone numbers, passwords, private account details, or identifying information shared in confidence.

2.2 — No account theft or phishing

Do not distribute malicious links, fake login pages, token or password theft attempts, harmful files, or instructions intended to compromise another person’s account or device.

2.3 — Account responsibility

Players are responsible for activity on their accounts to the extent they control them. Secure your account and notify staff if it is compromised. A player may provide evidence that an account was compromised when appealing a punishment.

2.4 — No punishment evasion through accounts

Do not use alternate accounts, another person’s account, VPNs, or outside coordination for the purpose of bypassing an active NovaSMP punishment. Ordinary use of a VPN is not a violation by itself.

2.5 — No pressure for personal information

Do not pressure players to reveal their real name, location, school, contact details, photos, or other personal information. Sharing your own information is your choice, but staff may remove exposed private information to protect safety.

3. Inappropriate content

3.1 — No explicit sexual content

Sexually explicit material, pornography, sexual images, and explicit sexual messages are prohibited on the Minecraft server and official Discord.

3.2 — Inappropriate builds, skins, names, and items

Sexually explicit builds, signs, pixel art, skins, usernames, item names, or other inappropriate representations are prohibited. Hate symbols and targeted discriminatory displays are also prohibited.

3.3 — No harmful or shocking media

Do not post graphic, disturbing, or otherwise seriously inappropriate media. Staff may remove content and escalate based on severity and platform rules.

3.4 — Report; do not redistribute

Do not repost prohibited content to prove it exists. Report the message or incident and provide relevant context. Staff should avoid redistributing explicit or private material.

4. Good-faith reports

Do not fabricate reports, edit evidence to mislead staff, encourage mass-reporting, or retaliate against someone for reporting a concern. Knowingly false reports intended to harm another player may be punished.

Gameplay Rules: Fair Play, Economy & Server Integrity

5. Fair play and allowed modifications

5.1 — Hacked clients and cheats

Using hacked clients, X-ray, kill aura, reach hacks, flight cheats, combat automation, or other unauthorized modifications that provide an unfair advantage is prohibited.

5.2 — Macros, bots, autoclickers, and automation

Do not use macros, scripts, bots, autoclickers, or automated tools to gain a prohibited advantage, automate combat, bypass server limits, or evade intended gameplay restrictions. Accessibility tools may be reviewed by leadership before use where they could affect fair play.

5.3 — External information advantages

Do not use prohibited tools or modifications to obtain hidden information or capabilities ordinary players are not intended to have, including unauthorized entity tracking or exploit-based map information.

5.4 — No exploit abuse

Do not knowingly abuse bugs, duplication glitches, rollback errors, permission mistakes, or unintended mechanics to gain items, currency, access, or other advantages. Report significant exploits privately to staff.

5.5 — No exploit distribution for abuse

Do not coordinate widespread abuse of an exploit or distribute instructions with the intent to damage server integrity, bypass restrictions, or harm the community.

6. Survival gameplay, PvP, raiding, and griefing

NovaSMP supports competitive survival gameplay. PvP, raiding, stealing, and ordinary griefing may be permitted unless a clearly published rule, event rule, or protected-area restriction says otherwise.

6.1 — PvP

Player-versus-player combat is permitted under the normal survival rules. There is no protected area at spawn. A specific event may have temporary restrictions only when leadership clearly announces the rule and identifies its boundaries in advance. Cheats, exploits, or harassment are never permitted as part of PvP.

6.2 — Raiding and stealing

Raiding bases and stealing items may be part of normal survival gameplay, including at spawn. Players should not assume a base, build, or belongings are protected unless leadership has explicitly announced a specific protection rule and its boundaries. Spawn itself is not protected.

6.3 — Griefing and protected locations

Griefing is permitted under the normal survival rules. There is no protected area at spawn. A separate event area or build is protected only if leadership clearly announces that protection and identifies the exact boundaries before the restriction takes effect.

6.4 — No spawn or event disruption

Spawn is not a protected area, so ordinary survival rules apply there. Event restrictions apply only to a specific event when leadership has announced the restriction and clearly identified its boundaries. Do not deliberately disrupt an official event in violation of its published event rules.

6.5 — Combat logging

Combat logging is prohibited only when a specific event or published server rule explicitly defines it as a violation. Staff must not invent a combat-logging rule after an incident.

6.6 — Important distinction

Ordinary in-game raiding or stealing is not the same as scamming during an agreed trade. Staff must apply the relevant rule rather than automatically treating all losses as violations.

7. Trading and economy

7.1 — No scamming

Deliberately deceiving another player in an agreed trade to obtain items, currency, or services is prohibited.

7.2 — Honor agreed trades

Before a trade, both players should understand what is being exchanged. Keep evidence of significant trades. Staff may be unable to resolve disputes when evidence is insufficient.

7.3 — No economy exploits

Do not exploit shops, currency systems, trading systems, rollback behavior, or unintended mechanics to generate unauthorized profits.

7.4 — No unauthorized real-money trading

Real-money transactions involving accounts, ranks, items, or currency are prohibited unless expressly authorized by NovaSMP leadership.

7.5 — No payment fraud or chargeback abuse

Do not use stolen payment methods, false payment claims, or deliberate chargebacks to obtain or keep NovaSMP goods or perks without paying. Genuine billing disputes should be raised through the official support process.

7.6 — No account or rank sales

Do not sell, trade, or transfer accounts, ranks, or paid perks unless leadership has expressly announced an approved transfer process.

7.7 — Trading disputes

A failed trade, misunderstanding, or disagreement is not automatically proof of scamming. Staff must establish deliberate deception before applying a scam punishment.

8. Server performance and integrity

8.1 — No intentional crashing or attacks

Deliberately crashing the Minecraft server, attacking its infrastructure, attempting unauthorized access, or intentionally making it unavailable is prohibited.

8.2 — No malicious lag machines

Do not deliberately construct or operate machines intended to cause severe lag, crash the server, or make it unplayable. Normal farms and redstone builds are not violations merely because they use resources; the issue is deliberate or clearly reckless server disruption.

8.3 — No abuse of server services

Do not exploit server commands, plugins, permissions, APIs, or network services to obtain unauthorized access, items, currency, or information.

8.4 — Report major bugs

Report serious issues involving duplication, currency, permissions, security, or server stability to staff. Do not repeatedly test a live exploit against other players.

Official Discord Rules

These rules supplement the player conduct and safety rules. Discord members must also follow Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

9. Discord conduct

9.1 — No harassment or targeted abuse

Do not use Discord to target, threaten, stalk, repeatedly humiliate, or coordinate harassment against members. This includes unwanted repeated insulting memes, images, nicknames, or direct messages.

9.2 — No spam or disruptive mentions

Do not flood channels, repeatedly ping users or roles, mass-mention members, or move spam between channels to evade moderation.

9.3 — No malicious links or files

Do not distribute phishing links, malware, token grabbers, harmful files, fake giveaways, or deceptive links.

9.4 — No doxxing or private information

Do not post another person’s private or identifying information without authorization. Do not threaten to expose private information.

9.5 — Channel restrictions

Use channels for their intended purposes and follow pinned instructions, slow mode, and moderator directions.

9.6 — No punishment evasion

Do not use alternate accounts, another person’s account, or outside coordination to bypass NovaSMP Discord restrictions.

9.7 — Staff spaces

Do not leak confidential staff discussions, private reports, security details, or internal evidence without authorization. This rule does not prevent a good-faith report of staff misconduct to an appropriate reviewer or Owner.

9.8 — Official links and announcements

Treat only links posted through designated official channels or verified staff announcements as official NovaSMP links. Staff must not use fake announcements or giveaways to mislead members.

9.9 — Off-platform conduct connected to NovaSMP

The staff team may review off-platform conduct only when it has a direct and meaningful connection to NovaSMP, such as coordinated harassment of NovaSMP members, impersonation of NovaSMP staff, or an attack on NovaSMP services. Staff authority does not extend to unrelated private activity.

Punishment Schedule

Important counting rule: Slurs are punished at 10 minutes per slur, added together. Ordinary swear words are 5 minutes per swear word, added together. These per-word mutes replace the old multi-day first-offense slur penalty. After the first warning, later violations follow the schedule. The serious-offense exception can override the warning-first default.

10. How punishments work

Universal first-offense rule: The first verified violation of any rule category normally receives a warning. This applies to every category in the schedule; a first offense may start with a stronger action only when the serious-offense exception applies. The warning must be logged so later violations can be counted. After the warning, use the later steps shown in the table for that same rule category. A warning under one rule category does not count as a prior offense under a different category.

A kick may be used to end an active disruption. For a normal first offense, the warning remains the disciplinary action; for a sufficiently serious offense, staff may bypass the warning under the serious-offense exception. Staff may also take immediate protective steps to remove harmful content or secure the server while reviewing an incident.

Repeat window: The normal repeat-offense window is 90 days for the same or closely related rule. Only valid, documented, relevant prior offenses count. If 90 days pass without another violation of that rule, the next offense normally starts again with a warning, unless the serious-offense exception applies. Cheating, serious exploits, ban evasion, doxxing, and serious safety violations may be reviewed over a longer period, and the serious-offense exception may apply to a first offense.

Serious-offense exception: The warning-first rule does not have to apply when an offense is serious enough to justify stronger first-time action. Based on reliable evidence and the risk involved, staff may skip the warning and use a later punishment step, an immediate ban, or a permanent-ban review where the schedule and severity support it. Examples may include credible real-world threats, doxxing, malicious server attacks or unauthorized access, phishing or account theft, severe targeted harassment, or conduct creating an immediate safety or server-security risk. Staff must record the evidence and explain why a warning was not sufficient. Staff may also immediately remove harmful content or temporarily restrict access to stop an active threat while the incident is reviewed. Do not use this exception for ordinary first-time mistakes or minor violations. Do not automatically stack every row when one incident falls under multiple rules; apply the most relevant primary rule and consider the rest when assessing severity.

11.1 — Notes on specific categories

  • Slurs: The first verified incident involving slurs normally receives a warning, unless the serious-offense exception applies. After that warning, each additional slur adds a 10-minute mute. Multiple slurs in one later incident are added together (for example, 3 slurs = 30 minutes). Slurs do not automatically trigger a multi-day ban. Threats or targeted harassment may be reviewed under their separate rules.
  • Ordinary swearing: Mild incidental profanity is not automatically punished. The first verified incident involving punishable profanity normally receives a warning, unless the serious-offense exception applies. After that warning, each clearly identifiable swear word adds a 5-minute mute; multiple words add together. Directed insults or harassment may also be handled under the harassment rule; do not count the same word twice under two profanity categories.
  • Harassment: Repeated unwanted images, memes, nicknames, or jokes aimed at one person can qualify when the person has asked for it to stop. Staff must review the context and evidence.
  • Threats: Staff must distinguish obvious in-game banter from credible threats. Credible real-world threats require immediate leadership escalation and appropriate safety procedures.
  • Scamming: A failed trade or disagreement is not proof. Staff must establish deliberate deception.
  • Raiding and stealing: These are allowed in ordinary survival areas unless a published protection rule applies. Do not punish ordinary raiding as scamming.
  • Permanent bans: Except for the immediate server-attack security action described above, permanent bans should receive leadership review and a documented reason. An appeal route should remain available unless doing so would create a safety or security risk.

12. Mute, kick, and ban procedure

  1. Stop the conduct: A kick or temporary mute may be used to stop an active disruption.
  2. Identify the rule: Record the rule number and a brief factual reason.
  3. Check history: Review whether a warning or later punishment for that same rule category is already recorded inside the repeat window.
  4. Choose action: For a first verified offense, normally issue a warning. Check the serious-offense exception above; if the conduct is severe enough, staff may bypass the warning and use a proportionate higher action. Otherwise, use the next step for that rule category.
  5. Record the action: For every warning, mute, ban, disciplinary kick, staff action, or appeal outcome, create a staff-log entry with the required details.
  6. Explain and appeal: Give the player a concise reason and appeal route when safe and practical.

Staff must not add time because a player argues, complains, or appeals. A new violation during an appeal may be treated separately if supported by evidence.

Staff Code of Conduct

This section applies to all staff roles, including Trial Mod, Mod, Admin, and Head Admin. These are the four staff ranks used by NovaSMP. The Owner is the server ownership authority and is subject to the staff conduct standards when acting in an official capacity.

13. Required staff standards

13.1 — Same rules, higher standard

Staff must follow every player rule. Staff status does not excuse harassment, hate speech, cheating, scams, threats, or other prohibited conduct.

13.2 — Professional communication

Staff must communicate respectfully and remain reasonably calm during disputes. Do not insult, humiliate, bait, provoke, or publicly shame players. Do not use staff status to win personal arguments.

13.3 — No targeted harassment

Staff must not repeatedly direct insulting memes, images, nicknames, jokes, or comments at a player after that player has made clear the behavior is unwanted. Staff must not use a staff announcement, role, or channel to embarrass a player.

13.4 — No permission abuse

Do not use commands, permissions, console access, moderation tools, or private information for personal benefit, retaliation, intimidation, entertainment, or unrelated purposes.

13.5 — No retaliation

Do not punish, threaten, demote, silence, or otherwise target a player or staff member because they reported you, criticized you respectfully, appealed a punishment, or declined to agree with you.

13.6 — Impartial enforcement

Apply rules consistently regardless of friendship, rivalry, rank, donations, popularity, or personal opinion. Friends and other staff are not exempt.

13.7 — Evidence and accurate records

For significant punishments, record the rule, reason, duration, evidence, time, and issuing staff member. Never fabricate, alter, conceal, or knowingly misrepresent evidence or punishment history.

13.8 — Stay within authority

Use only moderation powers explicitly granted to your role. Do not invent punishments, issue unauthorized refunds or items, promise exceptions, or extend durations out of anger.

13.9 — Conflict of interest

A staff member personally involved in a dispute must not be the sole decision-maker for a serious punishment or appeal arising from it. Preserve the evidence and refer the case to an uninvolved staff member or Admin.

13.10 — Confidentiality and privacy

Protect private reports, staff discussions, player information, credentials, security details, and internal evidence. Share information only with people who need it for legitimate staff work. Confidentiality must not be used to silence good-faith misconduct reports to leadership.

13.11 — No favoritism or unauthorized benefits

Do not ignore violations by friends, punish opponents more harshly, or use staff privileges to provide unauthorized items, currency, ranks, or advantages.

13.12 — No obstruction

Do not delete relevant evidence, pressure witnesses, coordinate false accounts, interfere with appeals, or obstruct a legitimate investigation.

13.13 — Activity and leave

Follow activity expectations published by leadership. Communicate planned absences through the designated staff channel. Do not fake activity or claim work you did not perform. Approved leave should be respected.

13.14 — Staff chat and internal conduct

Keep internal discussions constructive and relevant. Bullying, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized disclosure are prohibited.

13.15 — Trial staff

Trial staff are held to the same integrity standards. Trial status does not grant unrestricted authority. Leadership may extend, end, or revoke a trial based on conduct, performance, reliability, or permission misuse. Ending a trial is a staff decision separate from any player punishment.

13.16 — Resignation or removal

When staff leave the team, they must relinquish staff-only access, credentials, channels, and confidential information as directed. Former staff must not use retained access or information to disrupt NovaSMP.

13.16A — Leaving the NovaSMP community while holding a staff role

Any staff member—including a Trial Mod, Mod, Admin, or Head Admin—who voluntarily leaves the NovaSMP Minecraft server or official Discord community while holding a staff role is considered to have left the staff team. Their staff rank and permissions are removed. Returning to either community does not restore the rank. They must start a new staff application and be accepted before receiving any staff role again; their previous rank is not guaranteed. An approved temporary leave arranged with leadership in advance is not treated as leaving. Accidental kicks, disconnections, and temporary technical problems are not voluntary departures.

13.16B — Reapplication after leaving or removal

Former staff who left the community, resigned, or were removed must reapply if they want to return to the staff team, unless leadership documents a different process for a specific case. Reapplication does not erase prior staff records or guarantee the same rank. Leadership may require a new trial period, retraining, or a different rank. A person removed for serious misconduct may be declared ineligible for reapplication for a stated period or permanently, with the decision documented.

13.17 — Leadership accountability

Admins, the Head Admin, and the Owner must review serious staff complaints fairly, avoid conflicts of interest, document significant decisions, and never retaliate against good-faith reports. Leadership is subject to the same rules.

14. Staff misconduct response ladder

Staff discipline is separate from player punishment. The first verified staff-conduct violation normally receives a warning and documented reminder. Serious staff misconduct may bypass the warning and lead to immediate temporary suspension or removal of permissions while leadership reviews the evidence; the final outcome may include demotion or removal from staff. The decision and reason must be documented. Leadership must consider seriousness, authority held, intent, impact, evidence, prior staff history, and risk of further misuse. Temporary access safeguards are not automatically a final disciplinary finding.

15. Staff complaint review

  1. Reports should be sent privately to an Admin, Head Admin, or Owner who is not involved.
  2. If the complaint concerns the assigned reviewer, send it to an uninvolved Head Admin or Owner. If the complaint concerns the Head Admin, send it to the Owner.
  3. Preserve relevant logs, message links, screenshots, and statements.
  4. The accused staff member must not control the investigation or decide their own appeal.
  5. Temporary permission restrictions or suspension may be used to protect the investigation or community; this is not a final finding of guilt.
  6. Record the decision, reasons, and any staff consequence.
  7. Do not retaliate against the reporter or witnesses.

Staff Authority & Governance

16. Recommended authority boundaries

The four staff ranks are Trial Mod, Mod, Admin, and Head Admin. A title alone does not grant commands. The following is a recommended baseline; the Owner must publish the exact permissions and limits for each rank.

Staff level Recommended authority Must escalate
Trial Mod Answer questions, gather evidence, issue warnings, and report incidents. Mutes or kicks only when expressly authorized. Bans, serious harassment, threats, staff complaints, appeals, exploit investigations, or anything outside granted permissions
Mod Handle routine reports and issue warnings, kicks, mutes, and short temporary bans within assigned limits. Long bans, permanent bans, staff complaints, privacy/security incidents, or cases involving another staff member
Admin Manage routine moderation, review appeals when uninvolved, investigate staff conduct, and adjust permissions within Head Admin/Owner-approved limits. Head Admin/Owner conflicts, permanent bans, major security cases, and permanent policy changes

Important: Trial Mod, Mod, Admin, and Head Admin are the four staff ranks. The Owner is the server ownership authority, not an additional rank in this table. The Owner must publish each rank’s exact command limits and maximum punishment durations. No staff member may exceed those limits.

17. Rule changes require a two-thirds staff vote

17.1 — Required approval

Any permanent addition, removal, rewrite, clarification that changes enforcement, change to a punishment duration, change to staff authority, or change to this voting procedure itself must receive affirmative votes from at least two-thirds (2/3) of all active eligible staff members before it takes effect.

17.2 — How the threshold is calculated

The denominator is the full number of active eligible staff members for that vote, not merely the number who respond. The required number of yes votes is rounded up to the next whole person:

Required yes votes = ceiling(eligible active staff × 2 ÷ 3).

Examples: - 3 eligible staff: at least 2 yes votes. - 4 eligible staff: at least 3 yes votes. - 5 eligible staff: at least 4 yes votes. - 6 eligible staff: at least 4 yes votes. - 7 eligible staff: at least 5 yes votes. - 8 eligible staff: at least 6 yes votes.

Abstentions and no responses do not count as yes votes. A proposal that does not reach the threshold is not approved.

17.3 — Who may vote

All active staff members with an official staff role may vote, including Trial Mods if they are active staff. Staff on approved leave or under suspension are excluded for that vote. A person with a direct personal conflict may be excluded from that specific vote only when the conflict is documented and an uninvolved leadership member confirms the exclusion.

17.4 — Notice and voting period

The proposal must be posted in the designated staff channel with the exact wording, reason for the change, and proposed effective date. Staff must have at least 48 hours to review and vote. The voting period should normally remain open for 72 hours unless every eligible staff member has voted sooner.

17.5 — Recordkeeping

Keep a record of the final wording, eligible staff count, yes/no/abstention totals, approval calculation, date, and effective date. Publish the approved change in the official rules channel and update the version number.

17.6 — No unilateral permanent changes

No individual staff member, Admin, or Owner may permanently change the published rules, punishment schedule, two-thirds threshold, or voting procedure without the required two-thirds approval. Leadership may propose changes and organize the vote, but may not bypass the vote.

17.7 — Temporary emergency safeguards

To protect players or server stability, the Owner or an authorized Admin may impose a clearly labeled temporary restriction or emergency procedure. It must be limited to what is necessary, documented, announced to staff, and submitted for a staff vote if it is to become permanent. An emergency temporary rule expires after 72 hours unless approved under the two-thirds process. This does not prevent staff from taking immediate action under existing rules to stop a specific incident.

17.8 — Effective date and no retroactivity

An approved change takes effect only after it is published with an effective date. Do not apply a new or stricter rule to earlier conduct. If a change reduces a penalty or makes previously prohibited conduct allowed, leadership may review an ongoing punishment for fairness.

17.9 — Staff cannot be punished for voting honestly

No staff member may be threatened, demoted, muted, removed, or treated unfairly for voting yes, no, or abstaining in good faith. Vote manipulation, intimidation, or falsifying results is staff misconduct.

18. Decisions that are not rule amendments

Routine enforcement of an existing rule, correction of a mistaken punishment, a one-time event instruction, and a temporary case-specific safety action are not permanent rule amendments. They must still be within staff authority and documented where significant. A new standing rule or a change to how a rule is generally enforced requires the two-thirds vote.

Reports, Appeals & Moderation Records

19. Reporting a violation

When reporting an incident, provide: - The username and, if relevant, Discord user ID. - The rule number that may have been violated. - A short factual explanation of what happened. - Screenshots, recordings, message links, logs, or other relevant evidence when available. - Approximate time and location, when relevant. - Relevant context, including whether the behavior continued after a request to stop.

Do not fabricate evidence, encourage mass reports, or retaliate against the people involved. Reports should be sent through the official reporting channel or privately to the appropriate staff member.

20. Moderation records

Staff must create a log entry for every moderation action, including an informal or formal warning, mute, disciplinary kick, temporary or permanent ban, staff warning, staff suspension, staff demotion or removal, and any appeal decision, reversal, or correction. Log the action as soon as practical after it is issued. A warning must be logged because it counts when deciding whether a later violation is a repeat offense.

Each record must include: the player or staff member's username; Minecraft or Discord as the platform; the action type; the rule number or staff-policy section; the date and time; the duration or end date when applicable; the staff member who issued the action and their current rank; a concise factual reason; evidence or case link when available; whether this is a first or repeat offense; any serious-offense exception used and why; and the appeal/review outcome when one exists.

20.1 — Staff incident-log tool and storage

NovaSMP provides a separate staff incident-log page named staff-log/index.html. Staff should use it to enter and review warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, staff discipline, and appeal outcomes. The page stores entries in the browser on the device where it is opened. It is not a shared database and does not automatically synchronize between staff members. Export a JSON backup regularly and store it only in a private staff-controlled location approved by the Head Admin or Owner. Import backups when continuing records on another device.

Privacy and access: The staff log and its backups are internal staff records. Do not upload completed logs or backups to the public NovaSMP rules repository, public Discord channels, public file links, or public paste sites. Keep access limited to the staff members who need the records for moderation or review. Do not record passwords, IP addresses, home addresses, school details, or other unnecessary private information. The standalone HTML page itself has no secure login; do not treat a public link or browser storage as access control.

Do not fabricate, backdate, silently alter, or delete records to conceal an action. Correct a mistake with a clearly noted correction or appeal outcome so the history remains understandable. Restrict access to moderation logs to staff who need them for legitimate duties.

21. Player appeals

21.1 — Right to appeal

Players may appeal a warning, mute, kick, temporary ban, or permanent ban through the official NovaSMP appeal process.

21.2 — What to include

An appeal should identify the punishment, explain why it may be incorrect or excessive, and provide relevant evidence or context.

21.3 — Independent review

Where possible, an appeal should be reviewed by someone who did not issue the original punishment and is not personally involved in the dispute.

21.4 — Possible outcomes

The reviewer may uphold, reduce, remove, or correct a punishment. The outcome and reason should be recorded. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee removal.

21.5 — No retaliation

Players must not be punished merely for submitting an appeal or respectfully questioning a decision. Staff must not retaliate against appellants.

22. Final notice

These rules apply across the NovaSMP Minecraft server, official Discord, and official events. Repeated violations may lead to escalating punishments. Severe violations may receive immediate action. Staff must apply rules consistently, impartially, and within assigned authority.

The current approved version must be made available to players. Proposed amendments are not active until they receive the required two-thirds staff approval and are published with an effective date.

Play fairly. Respect the community. Protect NovaSMP.
